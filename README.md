# Madgrowth

Source for [www.madgrowth.io](https://www.madgrowth.io/).

Madgrowth helps leaders in tech with 5 to 10+ years of experience start a business of their own: a fractional practice, an advisory or services business, an agency or software. It's run by [Madalena Rugeroni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/madalenarugeroni), ex-Google, advisor and investor, who scaled startups from zero to $10M in revenue.

- [The Builder Diagnostic](https://www.madgrowth.io/diagnostic/): free, about 3 minutes, finds your Builder Archetype and the businesses that fit how you work
- [The Stack](https://www.madgrowth.io/stack/): AI playbooks that run in Claude to decide, position, price and launch
- [Startup go-to-market advisory](https://www.madgrowth.io/startups/): for founders from 0 to $1M in revenue
- [Guides](https://www.madgrowth.io/guides/) and [Claude skills for business](https://www.madgrowth.io/claude-skills/)
