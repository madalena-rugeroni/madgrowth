Where it stalls

You have the credibility. The offer is still missing.

You've shipped products, built teams and hit targets at companies people know. You want more control over your time and something you own. When you try to turn that into a business, four questions come up and stay open for months.

01

What do I sell?

Ten years of work could support a dozen businesses. Committing to one feels like closing off the rest.

02

Who is it for?

"I help companies grow" gives nobody a reason to call you. A buyer needs to recognize their problem and see why you're the one to fix it.

03

How do I package it?

Day rate, retainer, fixed-scope project or product. The same expertise earns very different money depending on the format.

04

What do I charge?

Most operators anchor on their last salary, price too low and end up with more work and thinner margins.

Why not just…

Options you've probably considered

…ask ChatGPT or Claude?

You'll get a reasonable answer to each question and no decision at the end. The Stack remembers your business across fifteen skills, applies the playbook for your business model and passes each result to the next skill.

…take a creator course?

Most start with building an audience. Senior operators get paid sooner by defining the offer and the price first, then reaching the small group who'll buy it.

…join a fractional marketplace?

Useful once you know exactly what you sell and to whom. Marketplaces take a cut of every engagement and expect you to pitch yourself.

…figure it out alone?

Possible. It's also why so many people think about this for a year or more and never launch.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to quit my job?

No. The system is built to run alongside a job, and most clients create their first offer while still employed.

What if I don't know what I'd sell?

Most people start there. The free Expertise Audit skill finds your three strongest angles in about ten minutes, using the career you already have.

I already use ChatGPT or Claude. Why pay for skills?

The Stack asks about your business once and remembers it across fifteen skills. Each skill applies the playbook for your business model, uses what the earlier skills produced and ends with a document you keep: an ICP, an offer, a price, a launch plan.

Fractional, services or software: does it matter which?

Not at the start. All three begin with what you sell, who buys it and what it costs. The skills use a different playbook for each, and the Go / No-Go scores the idea against your Builder Archetype before you commit months to it.

I have around 5 years of experience. Is that enough?

If you've owned an outcome, like a number, a team or a launch, yes. The Expertise Audit looks for the angle in the work you've done, whatever your title was.

Do I need a big audience?

No. The audience step builds inbound from a small, focused presence. A few hundred of the right people is enough to launch.

The Stack, The Audit or The Build?

They use the same system. The Stack is the fifteen skills on your own (299€ once). The Audit is my written diagnosis of what you have (599€). The Build is the skills plus me, 1:1, with a guarantee (1,200€/month). If you're unsure, book the free 15-minute call from The Build card.

Do I have to run the skills in order?

No. The first skill you run asks eight questions about your business, once. Every other skill reads those answers, so you can start with pricing, the landing page or the Go / No-Go. There's a recommended sequence, and you're free to skip around.
