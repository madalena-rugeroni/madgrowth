I'm Madalena Rugeroni, founder of Madgrowth. I help leaders in tech turn what they know into a business they own: a fractional role, an advisory practice, an agency or software. I also advise startup founders on go-to-market, from zero to their first $1M in revenue.

I've spent fifteen years building and scaling things: at Google, as a first-time founder, as a country manager, as a growth leader in B2B SaaS, and now as the owner of a small portfolio of internet businesses. Most of what I teach, I learned by getting it wrong first.

What I believe about building

Experienced operators usually have the credibility to go independent. Where they get stuck is the offer: what to sell, who buys it, how to package it and what to charge. That is the problem Madgrowth works on, either self-serve with AI agents that run in Claude, or 1:1 with me.

I don't believe in the VC rat race for most people. No big team, no big burn, no board meetings. A portfolio of small, leveraged businesses that pay for the freedom to keep building is a better life for most leaders in tech than the next title.

The cost of building has collapsed. Testing an idea used to take €50K and six months; now it takes an afternoon. Building stopped being the hard part. Validation and distribution are, and that is where I spend my time with clients.

The Builder Archetypes

I created the Builder Archetypes, a framework that sorts people into five types (Broadcaster, Advisor, Productizer, Venture Builder, Orchestrator) based on five dimensions: Engine, Bet, Tempo, Moat and Buyers. It's the basis of the free Builder Diagnostic, and it exists because the first question is never "what's a good business" but "which business fits how you work".
