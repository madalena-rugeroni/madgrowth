hi, I'm Madalena. I turn tech operators into owners.

Over the last decade, I've lived the tech operator trap.

Leading teams at Google. Scaling startups from zero to $10M in revenue. Six-figure salary, fancy titles, and zero ownership of my time.

I had built everything the playbook said to build. But my income still depended on one employer, my expertise was trapped inside someone else's company, and my time wasn't my own.

So I built a way out. Then I built Madgrowth to help others do the same. Today I advise and invest in startups and run a portfolio of internet businesses, including this practice and Mazo, my software company.

I work with leaders in tech who are done building for others and ready to build for themselves, whether that's a fractional role, a services business or software.

I've been where you are: the knowledge, the experience and the drive, with no clear path to make it work independently. That's the path I'll help you find.

What I work on

Experienced operators usually have the credibility to go independent. Where they get stuck is the offer: what to sell, who buys it, how to package it and what to charge. That's the part I work on.

  • The Builder Diagnostic, free: short scenarios that find your Builder Archetype and the business models that fit how you work.
  • The Stack, 299€: fifteen AI skills for Claude that position, package, price and launch your offer, with a playbook for each business model.
  • The Audit, 599€: my written diagnosis of your angle, positioning, offer, audience and launch plan, with a 90-day roadmap.
  • The Build, 1,200€ a month: three months 1:1 with me to position, package, price and launch.
  • Startup go-to-market advisory, 3,000€ a month: for founders from 0 to $1M in revenue.
