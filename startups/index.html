FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you only work with SaaS startups?

No. I work with B2B, B2C, marketplace and services-led startups, because the go-to-market fundamentals carry across. B2B SaaS founders also get Mazo for the work between sessions.

Is this hands-on execution?

It's advisory. We make the decisions together and I review what your team builds before it ships. If you need someone to run campaigns, an agency is a better fit.

How is this different from hiring a Head of Growth?

A senior GTM hire takes months to find and costs a full salary. This can start after a 15-minute call, and when you're ready to hire, we define the role together.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Three months. That's enough time to find what's capping revenue, change it and see whether the change worked. After that it's month to month.

Why only five founders?

So each founder works with someone who knows their numbers, their customers and what we decided last session.

What happens on the call?

15 minutes on where you are, what's stuck and whether this is the right help. You'll leave with the first thing to fix, whether or not we work together.
