FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you only work with SaaS startups?

No. B2B, B2C, marketplaces, services-led startups — the go-to-market fundamentals are the same. If you're B2B SaaS, Mazo is included for the work between sessions.

Is this hands-on execution?

It's advisory: we decide together and I review what you build before it ships, but your team does the building. If you need campaigns run for you, an agency is a better fit.

How is this different from hiring a Head of Growth?

A senior GTM hire takes months to find and costs a full salary. This starts after a 15-minute call. And when you are ready to hire, we define the role together.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Three months. That's how long it takes to diagnose what's capping revenue, change it, and see whether the change worked. After month three, it continues month to month.

Why only five founders?

So every founder gets someone who actually knows their numbers, their customers and what we decided last session.

What happens on the fit check?

15 minutes on where you are, what's stuck, and whether this is the right help. No pitch deck, no pressure.
