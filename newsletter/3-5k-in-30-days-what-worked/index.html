€3.5K in 30 days from a digital product: what actually worked

Madalena Rugeroni · Founder of Madgrowth · ex-Google · Sent 6 March 2025 · 3 min read

Originally sent as "3.5k€ in 30 days—here's what actually worked" to the From operator to owner newsletter.

A month ago, I set out to test a simple idea: could I build an income stream in 30 days, without a team, without ad spend, and without sacrificing my sanity?

Turns out I could. By the end of the first month, my course was generating €3.5K a month in revenue, and I wasn't glued to my screen 24/7 trying to make it happen.

What most people think you need

A massive audience (I didn't have one). Tons of capital (I spent less than €300). A full-time commitment (I worked on this alongside other projects). You don't need any of that. You do need a system that works without you constantly pushing it forward.

What actually worked

1. Build once, sell repeatedly

I didn't build a service that demands my time. I built a digital product I created once and can sell over and over. No manual sales, no constant launches. Most people overcomplicate this. They create offers that require ongoing involvement. The magic happens when you decouple time from income.

2. Automate the boring stuff

Email sequences for nurturing leads and converting them into buyers. Content repurposing so one idea fuels multiple platforms. Onboarding that delivers a great experience without me lifting a finger.

3. Start with an audience, not a product

Most people build a product and then try to sell it. I did the opposite. I started by sharing ideas, listening, and understanding what people were struggling with. When I built the course, I wasn't guessing. The result: a newsletter with a 71% open rate and sales that came in organically, without ads.

4. Daily content is long-term leverage

Content is a distribution channel. I committed to posting every day, and in one month my following grew 25%, I generated 150K+ impressions, and more people found the product organically. Most people give up after a few posts, but content compounds. One post today can bring in customers for months.

5. Community over cold selling

No one likes being pitched. Everyone loves finding insights that solve their problems. Instead of selling, I focused on helping. When people see you as a trusted source, they come to you ready to buy.

This first month was proof that it's easier than ever to turn expertise into income. The barriers are lower, the costs are minimal, and the leverage is real. The right time was yesterday. The second-best time is now.

Update, 2026: this course was later retired; what I learned building and selling it is now in The Stack and the guides.
