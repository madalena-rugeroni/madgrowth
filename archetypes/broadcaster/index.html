Builder Archetype

The Broadcaster

Your name is the distribution. You build in public, and demand comes to you.

Definition

The Broadcaster is one of the five Builder Archetypes in the Madgrowth Builder Diagnostic. It describes people best suited to creator-led businesses where your voice is the distribution: newsletters and media brands, cohort programs, audience-first launches and digital products.

How The Broadcaster works

When you say what everyone else is thinking, the right people show up in your inbox. Attention is your raw material, and unlike most operators, you enjoy the exposure.

The businesses that fit you are the ones where your voice is part of the product: audience-first launches, media-style brands and digital offers with demand in place before the product exists. Get visible first, then build the machine behind it.

What your full profile adds

If the diagnostic places you here, your full profile goes further: the specific businesses built for The Broadcaster, the ones that fight this wiring, the blind spot that stalls most people with this archetype, and how you work at your best and under stress. It also shows your secondary archetype, because most people are a blend.
