The Builder Diagnostic

The five Builder Archetypes

A Builder Archetype describes how you're wired to build a business: where your leverage comes from, how much risk you take and who you sell to. It tells you which businesses will fit you before you spend a year on the wrong one.

The short answer
  • There are five Builder Archetypes: The Broadcaster, The Advisor, The Productizer, The Venture Builder and The Orchestrator.
  • They come from the free Builder Diagnostic by Madgrowth, built for leaders in tech with 5 to 10+ years of experience.
  • Each archetype is a position on five dimensions: Engine, Bet, Tempo, Moat and Buyers.
  • Most people are a blend, so the diagnostic gives you a primary and a secondary archetype.

The Broadcaster

Your name is the distribution. You build in public, and demand comes to you.

The Advisor

A few clients who pay properly for judgment that took a decade to build.

The Productizer

Package once, sell many times. You turn what you know into things that work without you.

The Venture Builder

The long game. You'd rather spend two years building something that could be worth twenty.

The Orchestrator

You build the machine that does the work: teams, systems and leverage.

The five dimensions behind the archetypes

The diagnostic places you on five dimensions. Neither end is better. They describe where you get leverage and what kind of business will feel natural to run.

DimensionOne endThe other end
EngineYouThe machine
BetReversibleAll-in
TempoLong gameThis week
MoatWhat you knowWho knows you
BuyersA handfulThousands
  • Engine: whether the business runs on you (your name, your judgment) or on a machine (a team, a system, software).
  • Bet: whether you prefer reversible steps with evidence first, or going all-in when you believe in something.
  • Tempo: whether you play long games that compound, or work in weekly sprints.
  • Moat: whether people pay for what you know, or come to you because of who knows you.
  • Buyers: whether you want a handful of high-trust clients, or thousands of customers.

Why your archetype matters before you pick a business

Senior operators rarely fail for lack of skill. They fail by picking a model that fights how they work: an Advisor launching a low-ticket course that needs thousands of buyers, or a Venture Builder stuck in a services business capped by their calendar. Knowing your archetype first narrows the options to the ones you'll actually keep running.

Questions people ask

What are the Builder Archetypes?

The Builder Archetypes are five profiles from the Madgrowth Builder Diagnostic that describe how someone is wired to build a business: The Broadcaster, The Advisor, The Productizer, The Venture Builder and The Orchestrator. Each one points to the business models that fit how that person works.

Who created the Builder Archetypes?

Madalena Rugeroni created the Builder Archetypes and the Builder Diagnostic for Madgrowth, for leaders in tech deciding between a fractional role, a services business or software.

How is a Builder Archetype calculated?

The diagnostic asks short work scenarios and scores the answers on five dimensions: Engine, Bet, Tempo, Moat and Buyers. Your scores are matched to the closest archetype profile, and the second closest becomes your secondary archetype.
