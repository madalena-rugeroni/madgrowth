Builder Archetype

The Productizer

Package once, sell many times. You turn what you know into things that work without you.

Definition

The Productizer is one of the five Builder Archetypes in the Madgrowth Builder Diagnostic. It describes people best suited to packaged, repeatable offers sold many times: toolkits and templates, self-serve courses and productized services with a fixed scope and price.

How The Productizer works

You're wired to ship. While others are still naming the planning doc, you have a v1 in someone's hands. Your instinct is to turn messy, bespoke work into something with edges: a template, a system, a fixed price. That instinct is most of the job.

The businesses that fit you are packaged and repeatable: toolkits and templates people buy off the shelf, self-serve courses with no delivery time and productized services with a fixed scope and price. Revenue should grow with copies sold, and you already sense that.

What your full profile adds

If the diagnostic places you here, your full profile goes further: the specific businesses built for The Productizer, the ones that fight this wiring, the blind spot that stalls most people with this archetype, and how you work at your best and under stress. It also shows your secondary archetype, because most people are a blend.
