Builder Archetype

The Advisor

A few clients who pay properly for judgment that took a decade to build.

Definition

The Advisor is one of the five Builder Archetypes in the Madgrowth Builder Diagnostic. It describes people best suited to high-ticket businesses that sell senior judgment to a few clients: fractional leadership, high-ticket consulting, advisory retainers and fixed-price audits.

How The Advisor works

You're wired for depth: fewer people, harder problems, more trust. When a genuinely ambiguous call needs making, you're the calmest person in the room and the one they call back. That trait is an asset, and most operators never learn to price it.

The businesses that fit you sell judgment: fractional leadership, high-ticket consulting with a sharp named offer, advisory retainers or a paid teardown at a fixed price. Fifteen people with budget who know exactly what you fix is enough.

What your full profile adds

If the diagnostic places you here, your full profile goes further: the specific businesses built for The Advisor, the ones that fight this wiring, the blind spot that stalls most people with this archetype, and how you work at your best and under stress. It also shows your secondary archetype, because most people are a blend.
