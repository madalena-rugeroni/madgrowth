Guides for leaders in tech building a business of their own

Plain answers to the questions that stop experienced operators: what to sell, which business to build, and how to launch it, whether that's software, an audience, an agency, digital products or a fractional practice.

Madalena Rugeroni · Founder of Madgrowth · ex-Google

What to sell after leaving a tech job

Sell an outcome you've delivered more than once to a buyer who already pays for it. How to find your angle after a tech job, test it and avoid the traps.

Fractional, consulting or software: which business should you start after tech?

Fractional and consulting work pay within weeks; software pays later but grows past your hours. How ex tech leaders choose, with a side-by-side comparison.

How to build a micro SaaS from your tech career

Build a micro SaaS from a workflow you know: validate with pre-sales, price it for B2B and win your first 10 customers. Ideas, AI MVPs and real timelines.

How to build an audience business from your expertise

Build an audience business: pick a narrow beat from your career, one platform and a weekly cadence, then sell services or products before subscriptions.

How to start an agency after a career in tech

To start an agency, pick one client type and outcome, sell a productized offer first, price for margin and hand delivery to a small senior team and systems.

How to sell digital products from your expertise

Sell digital products by choosing a format your buyer already pays for, pre-selling it, then doing the distribution. Formats, pricing and platforms compared.

How to become a fractional executive

Become a fractional executive by packaging one problem you've solved as a monthly role with set days and outcomes, then selling it to your network first.

How to price fractional and consulting services

Price fractional services from the cost of the problem and the hire it replaces. Day rate vs retainer vs value pricing, with European day rates in euros.

How to create a Claude skill: folder structure, a SKILL.md example to copy, descriptions Claude triggers on, and installing it in Claude Code or the app.
